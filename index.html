A DOWNRIVER JOURNEY

Craft aged and barreled with care

In the early 1800s, merchants bought Kentucky whisky and sent it downriver to New Orleans. The two or three month journey changed the flavor and won over a French-speaking, cognac-drinking city.

Left Bank recreates that history. The bourbon is aged at least two years on the Ohio River, finished with added oak staves during its barge journey, then married in batches of 4 to 8 barrels and bottled without chill filtration. Every batch is barreled and bottled by hand.

How ordering works

Checkout stays on leftbankbourbon.com. Behind it, AccelPay routes every order to a licensed retailer in the buyer's state.
