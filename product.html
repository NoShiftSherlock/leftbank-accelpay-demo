What this demo shows

  • This is leftbankbourbon.com with the AccelPay cart in place of the Bottle Nexus buy button, so bottles sell on your own domain. It works on your Wix site as it is.
  • Every order is sold and fulfilled by a licensed retailer in the buyer's state, from a network of 3,500+ licensed retail locations reaching 40+ states.
  • No retailer in our network stocks Left Bank today, so every route here is a demo route, connected at launch. Prices shown are your current online prices. Licensed retailers set the final retail price.
  • Instant delivery runs through Gopuff (about 30 minutes) and DoorDash from local retailers, wherever a bottle is stocked nearby.
  • Launch takes about one week, with no per-order charge to Left Bank.
  • Reordering is built in. We track depletions at each retailer and flag reorders with suggested POs when stock runs low. For brands distributed by Southern Glazer's, we're setting up a pilot to send those reorders straight into Proof.
  • Brands that switch their white-label store to our plugins (the AccelPay or LiquidCommerce cart, or our native Shopify integration) automatically receive advertising credit on ReserveBar.com and Minibar.
  • Every brand gets a ReserveBar.com listing. Left Bank is not on ReserveBar.com today.
  • "Demo route" retailers, fees and tax are illustrative. No order is ever placed.
